About the Project

AI support for practical agricultural planning

The platform combines district selection, soil type, season, land area, live weather data, and a Random Forest model to recommend a suitable crop for Tamil Nadu farming conditions.

It helps farmers and administrators compare crop suitability, expected yield, likely profit, and sowing windows through a clear digital service interface.

Technology Foundation

  • Scikit-learn Random Forest model
  • OpenWeatherMap weather service
  • MongoDB Atlas prediction history
  • Validated inputs and environment-based configuration
